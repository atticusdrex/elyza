elyza.surrogate.gp package#

Gaussian Process regression surrogate. GaussianProcess pairs a pluggable kernel with a pluggable mean, fits hyperparameters by maximizing the log-marginal-likelihood, and supports incremental low-rank (block Cholesky) updates so new data can be ingested without a full refit.
