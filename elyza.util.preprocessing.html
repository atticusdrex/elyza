elyza.util.preprocessing module#

Feature preprocessing and transformation classes.

Provides scikit-learn-style fit/transform/fit_transform classes for standardizing inputs (StandardScaler), decorrelating inputs via an SVD-based whitening transform (OrthonormalScaler), mapping inputs into a kernel-feature space (KernelFeatures), and expanding inputs into polynomial feature bases (PolynomialFeatures).
